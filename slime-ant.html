Slime Mold & Ant Colony Path Optimization

Languages: Python
Technologies: Pygame, NumPy
GitHub: link

I was first introduced to the intersection of computer science and natural processes through the YouTube channel Pezzza's Work. I was fascinated by the emergent behavior arising from simple programmed interactions, so I built my own versions of these simulations.

Using Pygame as a visual interface, I successfully replicated the path optimization behavior of ant colonies. By slightly modifying the simulation parameters I could similarly replicate slime mold growth. While I got the emergent behavior I was after, both simulations were unfortunately single-threaded and slow due to my unfamiliarity with parallel processing at the time — something I hope to revisit.
