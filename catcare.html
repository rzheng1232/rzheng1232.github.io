CATCare

Best Beginner Hack — HackIllinois 2026

Award: Best Beginner Hack @ HackIllinois 2026
Domain: Multi-agent AI, Computer Vision, App Development
Technologies: FastAPI, Gemini, PyTorch, LightGlue, Firebase

CATCare is a multi-agent AI system built at HackIllinois 2026 that enables hands-free inspection of heavy equipment — winning Best Beginner Hack at the event.

The system uses a pipeline of coordinated AI agents to automate equipment inspection workflows. A computer vision module powered by PyTorch and LightGlue performs visual feature matching and anomaly detection on equipment images. A Gemini-backed reasoning agent interprets the visual findings and generates structured inspection reports. The backend is served via FastAPI with Firebase handling data persistence and authentication.

The hands-free design targets environments where operators can't safely interact with a screen — the system takes images, runs the full inspection pipeline, and surfaces actionable findings automatically.
