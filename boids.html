Boid's Algorithm Simulation

Languages: Python
Technologies: Pygame
GitHub: link

This was a quick exploration into how genetic algorithms play a role in swarming & flocking behaviors. I can't emphasize enough how amazing emergent behaviors are: by simply specifying 3 simple rules — Separation, Alignment, and Cohesion — these complex and beautiful patterns appear.

Side note: I was able to figure out multiprocessing and effective distribution of work in this project, so I was able to simulate many more particles.
