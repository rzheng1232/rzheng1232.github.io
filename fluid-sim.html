Particle Fluid Simulation

Languages: Python
Technologies: Pygame, NumPy
GitHub: link

My interest in fluid simulation primarily arose after seeing videos by Sebastian Lague on the topic. Although I had never touched the subject before, I decided to take it on as a summer challenge. I started by reading Wikipedia and various online sources to understand the underlying mathematics of fluid dynamics.

Since I lacked a lot of the mathematical foundation at the time, I approached the simulation using Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (SPH), calculating particle movement through pressure/density gradients and viscosity forces. Although not the most efficient method, I was still able to produce convincing fluid-like behavior. In the future I'd like to revisit this with the Lattice-Boltzmann method.
