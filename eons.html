EONS & Spiking Neural Networks

F1Tenth Autonomous Racing — TENNLab @ UIUC

Domain: Neuromorphic Computing, Robotics, Evolutionary Algorithms
Technologies: Python, EONS, Spiking Neural Networks, F1Tenth simulator
Organization: TENNLab @ UIUC

As part of TENNLab at UIUC, I applied the EONS (Evolutionary Optimization for Neuromorphic Systems) framework to improve autonomous navigation on the F1Tenth racing platform, achieving a 35% improvement in navigation performance over the baseline.

EONS uses evolutionary algorithms to train spiking neural networks (SNNs) — networks of integrate-and-fire neurons that communicate via sparse spike events rather than continuous activations. The evolutionary process optimizes both the network topology and synaptic weights simultaneously, producing controllers well-suited to the low-latency, energy-efficient constraints of neuromorphic hardware.

The F1Tenth simulator provides a realistic environment for testing wall-following and obstacle avoidance behaviors. The evolved SNN controllers were evaluated on lap times, collision rate, and trajectory smoothness.
